# Changelog

## Unreleased

- **The walk shook in place by barrels and steam vents** (Caverns 1: 10 s at an exploding barrel at 2 health): the route went through the hazard (no way around) while the fight stepped out of it every frame. Now stepping out that fights the walk for a second, a side detour that gets nowhere along the route for 2 s, or a route with no way around lets the walk go through the hazards within 160 units for 8 s (log `Fight: stepping out of ... fights the walk ...: walking through it`, `Route: no way past ... to the side ...: through it`). Putting the walk ahead now goes on along the route to the first floor (up to 4096 units) instead of into the air (Delta 4 skipped most of the map, Hell died 73 times in its lava pit); a hunt ends after 5 minutes without a kill (log `Fight: hunt: no kill in ...`).
- **Walking loops and pits** (Delta Labs 1 ran out of time): three detours at one spot within 4 minutes put the walk ahead (log `Walk: going round in a loop at ...`; it walked from an elevator to a corner and back for an hour). An elevator is called to the floor the next 800 units of route go to, not the floor it is already at. Give-ups count in the same run too, so god mode starts again when the route passes the place again (log `Fight: a run gave up near ...`; the run had died 26 times in the same kill pit). The give-up line now logs where the deaths were.
- **The walk remembers between runs**: every spot where it had to be put ahead, every death and every give-up (3 deaths at a place) is appended at once to `%LOCALAPPDATA%\castle-doom3\walkmemory\<map>.txt` (a run that times out keeps what it learned). A later run is put ahead at a remembered stuck spot after 2.5 s instead of 10, without trying doors and elevators there (log `Walk: stuck at a remembered spot ...`), and goes into god mode 300 units before a place an earlier run gave up at, for 900 units (log `Fight: a run gave up near ...`), instead of dying there three times again. The load logs `Walk memory: <map>: N stuck spots, N deaths, N give-ups from earlier runs`; the walk's summary counts what was used. Env `CD3_WALK_MEMORY=0` doesn't use it (it is still written); delete the file to start over. Test map: 101 s, then 80 s with its 3 stuck spots remembered; with 3 forced deaths 215 s, then 73 s without a death.
- **The bots turn the view smoothly** (walking, noclip routes, fights): the view was set straight at the goal every frame, so it snapped at every bend of the route and, at corners, swung between a monster and the route as the monster went in and out of sight. `TViewPlay.TurnViewTo` eases the turn (240 degrees a second walking, 540 aiming, 200 and a 10 degree dead band within 128 units of the target), the bot fires only once the aim is within 12 degrees (or the monster is within 96), and after losing sight of a monster it keeps looking at it for 0.8 s. Routes log `Route: view turns: N frames, N degrees a frame, N over 10 degrees, N reversals ...` and `View: shaking at <feet> (... turned by walk aim ...)` where the view turns back and forth 8 times within 2 s; env `CD3_SMOOTH_TURN=0` snaps like before. Test map: 270 to 330 reversals of turns over 2 degrees, now 9 to 13; Mars City 2 on foot: 1792 reversals and 42 shaking places with smoothing alone, now 642 and 6 (53 kills, 3 deaths, every shown monster dead).
- **Walking: falls, ledges, low bars and corners**: a fall of 400 units below the route and the last floor is caught before the fight step (Mars City 1: 574 falls, now 2), and the bot is put ahead onto a floor with headroom; when stuck it jumps onto ledges 16 to 68 up, crouches on flat ground, and takes a detour along the AAS route (at most twice per place; log `Walk: around the corner at ...`). Alpha Labs 3: 52 stuck spots, 1717 falls, 71 kills, now 36, 6, 101.
- **The hunt finds hidden monsters and punches with the fists**: after the shown ones it walks to the spawn of each never shown monster (its trigger is usually there) and wakes it if it is still hidden (log `Fight: hunt: to the place of ...`, `... still hidden ...: woken, as its trigger would`); with only the fists left it closes in instead of backing off (Mars City 2 left three machine gunners alive that way). The hunt's time is 25 minutes.
- **Walking the routes on foot** (console `route_walk 1`, `tools/tour_maps.ps1 -Walk`): instead of noclip the route is walked at Doom 3's run speed (220) with collisions and gravity; doors open when touched, the route's point goes on only while the feet keep up. Where it can't go on for 3 s it uses what is near (a door that touching doesn't open is opened, with why: locked, needs a key, `no_touch`, disabled; an elevator is called to the floor the route goes to, twice at most), after 10 s it logs the place (`Walk: stuck at ... (a wall between / N up / N down (fell off) / blocked, on the way to <goal>)`) and is put ahead along the route onto a floor that is not in a hurt volume (further each time it sticks again: the AAS's gaps); a fall through the floor is logged and put back on the route (`Walk: fell through the floor near ...`). The summary: `Walk: summary: N stuck spots, N falls through the floor, N doors opened for the walk, N elevators called; <places>`. Mars City Underground, Alpha Labs 1, CPU and Administration fought and hunted on foot (2026-11-22, `walk_doom3/`, `walk_doom3b/`, `walk_doom3c/`): all reach the next map; 141, 96, 85 and 39 stuck places (mostly 73 to 100 units up, the AAS's jumps and ledges the walk can't climb, and corners cut by the straight line).
- **The view went black after loading a save** (walking runs, a tenth of the screenshots): the step up's and the landing's view dips kept their times from before the death, in the restored clock's future, so the view moved up to 12000 units off the eye during rendering. They are reset with the player and ignore times ahead of the clock.
- **Doom 3's HUD is loaded again with every map**, like Doom 3: its DEAD window ("BIOSIGNAL LOST") and the red border stayed on for the rest of the run after the first death.
- **Fight saves only on a floor and outside hurt volumes**: a save made while falling into a kill volume after giving up died on every load (Mars City Underground: 315 deaths in a loop).

Hell's lava hurt volume ends at the floor: the trigger test's feet are 1 unit up.

- **The grabber catches projectiles and small monsters** (Resurrection of Evil): a monster's fireball in reach is held in front of the eye and thrown back as the player's (`def_damage` + `_catch` when the def exists; log `Grabber catches ...`, `Grabber throws ...`); lost souls, trites, forgotten ones, cherubs and ticks are held still and die thrown (`damage_suicide`), Grabber.cpp's `grabbableAI`.
- **Kills and bosses in the event checks, performance budgets** (roadmap "After the playthroughs" items 8 to 10): a monster's death logs `Killed <name> (<class>, <damage def>)`; `tools/map_events.py` counts kills and checks boss kills (`monster_boss_*`) as key events, and takes a fighting run's level end from the first load of another map (it reloads the same map from its saves after a death). `tools/map_perf.py` keeps a run's per-map numbers as a baseline and flags maps past their budget; `tools/tour_maps.ps1` checks both when the baselines exist. commoutside's fighting run, which used to reach the 40 minute cap, finishes (120 kills, 811 s); its deaths are by the electrified track's `noGod` damage. Test map reproductions of the day's bugs: `static_scripted_1`, `damage_test_lava`. - **The player body aims with the view** (torso aiming): Doom 3's player anims list three md5anim files (aim down, straight, up: `pistol_aim models/.../pistol_aim_down.md5anim, pistol_aim.md5anim, pistol_aim_up.md5anim`), blended by the view's pitch (idPlayer::AdjustBodyAngles, `SetSyncedAnimWeight` on the torso and legs); only the first was read, so in third person the retail body always aimed down. Model decls keep the other files (`<anim>#1`, `#2`), `TD3Md5Instance.SyncWeights` blends their parent-relative joints; the default (1, 0, 0) plays the first file like Doom 3, so other characters are unchanged.
- **Shader parameters on md5 models**: an md5 model's materials read its entity's `_color` / `shaderParmN` and what scripts set (`setShaderParm`, `setShaderParms`, `setColor`, `getShaderParm`, `getColor`; the head too), like brush entities: fades and transformations that stamp `parm4` with the time (`1-death_fade[(time+Parm4)*.5]`: zombified heads, Resurrection of Evil's hunters and artifact). The `setShaderParm` events left as unsupported on erebus6, phobos1 to 4, deltax, monorail and delta1 went to md5 models.
- **The skybox's top and bottom fit its sides** (roadmap "After the playthroughs" item 6): Doom 3's camera cube images were given to CGE's background as they are; its `_up` has `_forward` along its bottom edge, CGE's top face wants the front face (Doom 3's left) there, so the clouds broke at the cube's edges (commoutside, every map with a sky). The top is turned a quarter counter-clockwise, the bottom clockwise (the port's image program `castlecubeface( n, image )`; env `CD3_SKY_FACES` to try others), found from the images' matching edges.

- **`func_beam`, `func_securitycamera`, `trigger_flashlight`** (roadmap "After the playthroughs" items 3 and 4): beams are quads from the entity to its target turned to the view each frame (idRenderModelBeam), with the skin's material and the `_color` as vertex colors (proc surfaces can carry vertex colors now), both ends brush entities scripts move (monorail's scanning lasers, delta2b's bound beams, alphalabs1's arc through the high-voltage gate); activation toggles them. Flashlight triggers fire when the lit flashlight's beam reaches them (any flashlight: the weapon, the BFG armor one), not when walked into. `info_portalsky` is not done (it needs a render pass with its own portal visibility).
- **Static entities a map script names are no longer merged**: the merge only looked for names in `maps/<map>.script`, but the retail maps' scripts are `script/map_*.script` (named unlike their maps: `marscity1`, `caves`), so a `func_static` a script showed, hid, bound or moved could be merged and the script lost it (commoutside's `goofyarm_4`). Every `$name` and quoted string of every script file now keeps its entity apart (commoutside: 282 merged entities -> 281).

They also stop the shadow light from shining through walls. Grouped per area, enabled for visible areas and the areas the shadow light reaches.
- **`noshadows`**: materials with it don't cast (X3D `shadowCaster`), lights or light shaders with it are never the shadow casting light.
- Test level: solid brushes for walls, floors, ceilings, pillars and the balcony in its `.cm`.
- 269 checks (brush polyhedra are closed, insets, casters of the test level, `noshadows`); CI checks the casters are built.

Still one shadow casting light at a time (CGE's shadow volumes).

## 0.8.0

Material stages.

- Test level: scrolling terminal screens, pulsing ceiling panels, a skylight in room B.

## 0.7.0

Shadows.

- **Stencil shadows** with CGE's shadow volumes: every frame the strongest light at the player (lights in the player's area first, ambient lights never) casts shadows of brush entities (doors, crates, movers), static models and md5 models (up to 1500 triangles). Area geometry receives shadows.
- Console `r_shadows [0|1]`; screenshot log lines name the shadow casting light.
- 213 checks; CI checks the shadow light is chosen.

Known limitation: one shadow casting light at a time (CGE's shadow volumes), and walls don't cast shadows.

## 0.6.0

Light volumes.

- **Doom 3 light volumes**: lights don't fade with distance any more; like Doom 3 each lights a box (point lights, `light_radius`, `rotation`) or a frustum (projected lights, `light_target` / `light_right` / `light_up`, `light_start` / `light_end`), mapped to the light's texture space like `lightProject`, shaped by projection and falloff curves. Implemented as a per-light CGE shader effect (`PLUG_light_scale`) reading the fragment's world position.
- Lights without a `texture` use Doom 3's `lights/defaultPointLight` / `lights/defaultProjectedLight` when the game data declares them.
- `shaderParm4` is the light shader's time offset, like Doom 3.
- Console `r_lightVolumes [0|1]` reloads the map with plain spherical lights for comparison.
- **Fixed**: a shut door was hidden by portal visibility when seen from the room on its other side (it now belongs to the areas on both sides of its portal).
- Test level: a projected window light, Doom 3-style default light shaders and light images.
- 210 checks (light texture space, projected lights, shapes).

Known limitation: projection and falloff images are approximated by analytic shapes chosen from the image name (CGE doesn't bind textures referenced only by light effects).

## 0.5.0

Light shaders.

- **Material expressions and tables** (`Doom3Expressions`): `time`, `parm0-11`, `global0-7`, `table[...]` lookups (`snap`, `clamp`, wrapping and interpolation like Doom 3), arithmetic, comparisons, `&&` / `||`, with Doom 3's operator priorities.
- **Animated lights**: light shaders' color registers (`red`, `green`, `blue`, `rgb`, `rgba`, `color`, `colored`) are evaluated every frame with the light's `_color` and `shaderParm`s, so strobes, flickers and pulses work. A declared shader without color registers is white, an undeclared one uses `_color`, like Doom 3.
- Console `lights` lists every light's current color.
- Test level: a flickering (snap table), a pulsing (sine table) and a strobing light.
- 193 checks (expressions, tables, light shaders); CI checks that the pulsing light changes.

## 0.4.0

Portal visibility.

- **Portal visibility** like Doom 3's renderer: from the camera's area through the inter-area portals, each portal clipped by the view volume and narrowing it. Areas (now one scene each), brush entities, static and md5 models of hidden areas aren't drawn.
- **Doors close portals** while they are shut, so nothing behind a closed door is drawn.
- **Lights** are switched off while none of the areas they reach is visible (a flood through the portals within the light's radius).
- Console: `r_usePortals [0|1]`, `portals`; screenshot log lines show the visible areas. `TDoom3Map.PortalCulling`.
- Fixed: `notarget` didn't stop a monster that was already attacking.
- 162 parser and visibility checks; CI checks the visible areas through the door.

## 0.3.0

- Test level: LWO barrels, ASE terminals with glowing screens, a `.cm` with an invisible clip wall and a monster clip strip.
- 147 parser checks (from 102); CI checks that static and collision models load.
